# Pynapli Community Guidelines

Last updated: 2026-04-24

## Purpose

These guidelines define the behavior Pynapli expects from members and the rules moderators enforce.

Pynapli is a social lifestyle app for open-minded adults. The community works only when people are honest, respectful, privacy-conscious, and consent-forward.

## Core Principles

1. Adults only.
2. Consent is required.
3. Boundaries are respected.
4. Privacy is protected.
5. No harassment.
6. No scams or spam.
7. No paid sexual services.
8. Public surfaces stay app-store-safe.
9. Reports are taken seriously.

## Adults Only

Pynapli is for adults 18+.

Not allowed:

- Minors using the app.
- Content involving minors in any sexual or suggestive context.
- Joking or roleplay about minors in a sexual context.
- Helping someone underage access the app.

Confirmed minor-related sexual content results in immediate removal and escalation to appropriate authorities where required.

## Consent And Boundaries

Members must respect consent and boundaries.

Not allowed:

- Pressuring someone after they say no.
- Ignoring stated boundaries.
- Sharing intimate content without permission.
- Adding a linked partner to a chat without required consent.
- Misrepresenting whether a partner is involved.
- Continuing contact after being blocked or asked to stop.

Consent can change or be withdrawn at any time.

## Privacy

Do not expose another member's identity, location, photos, messages, or lifestyle participation without permission.

Not allowed:

- Doxxing.
- Sharing screenshots to shame or identify someone.
- Reposting private album content.
- Sharing someone's workplace, address, phone, social profiles, or legal name without permission.
- Outing someone as a Pynapli member.

## Public Content Rules

Public surfaces include:

- Grid photos
- Swipe photos
- Public profile photos
- Bios
- Prompts
- Public tags
- Playing Tonight public notes/photos

Public content must be app-store-safe.

Not allowed publicly:

- Nudity
- Explicit sexual imagery
- Graphic sexual descriptions
- Solicitation for paid sexual services
- Escort-style ads
- Hate, threats, harassment
- Doxxing
- Illegal activity

## Private Content Rules

Private content includes:

- Private albums
- Chat media
- Expiring photos
- Message threads

Private content is still subject to rules.

Not allowed:

- Minors or suspected minors
- Non-consensual intimate content
- Threats
- Doxxing
- Illegal exploitation
- Scam/fraud content
- Paid sexual service solicitation

Private album access can be revoked anytime.

## No Paid Sexual Services

Pynapli is not a marketplace for paid sexual services.

Not allowed:

- Escorting
- Sugar arrangements
- Paid meetups for sexual services
- Offering money or gifts for sexual access
- Advertising commercial sexual services

## No Harassment Or Hate

Not allowed:

- Threats
- Abuse
- Slurs
- Hate based on protected characteristics
- Repeated unwanted messages
- Sexual harassment
- Mocking or shaming someone's identity, body, boundaries, or relationship structure

## Authenticity

Members should represent themselves honestly.

Not allowed:

- Fake profiles
- Impersonation
- Catfishing
- Stolen photos
- Misrepresenting age
- Misrepresenting partner consent
- Creating accounts to evade bans

## Spam And Scams

Not allowed:

- Mass messaging
- Repeated identical messages
- Link spam
- Phishing
- Crypto/investment scams
- Off-platform scam funnels
- Automated scraping
- Fake verification/payment requests

## Partner Linking

Partner linking is optional and revocable.

Rules:

- Both users must consent to link.
- Either user can unlink.
- A linked partner does not control your account.
- A linked partner does not automatically access your private albums.
- A linked partner is invited into chats only through consent-based invite flow.

## Events And Venues

Members and venue partners must follow the same rules.

Not allowed:

- Misleading event promotion
- Unsafe venue promotion
- Paid sexual service solicitation
- Sharing attendee identities without consent
- Harassment at or around events

## Reporting

Report content or behavior that violates these guidelines.

Report categories include:

- Underage user
- Harassment or threats
- Explicit public content
- Spam or fake profile
- Impersonation
- Paid sexual services
- Non-consensual content
- Private info/doxxing
- Other safety issue

Blocking is available separately from reporting.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, Pynapli may:

- Remove content
- Warn a user
- Limit account features
- Require verification
- Suspend an account
- Ban an account
- Preserve evidence
- Report severe illegal content where required

## Appeals

Some enforcement actions can be appealed.

General appeal window:

- 30 days

Severe cases such as confirmed CSAM, confirmed fraud, or certain legal escalations may not be appealable.

## Do Not Forget

Pynapli is built for adults, privacy, consent, and trust. If a behavior makes people less safe or less willing to be authentic, it probably does not belong here.
