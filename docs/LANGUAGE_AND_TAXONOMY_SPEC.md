# Pynapli Language And Taxonomy Spec

Last updated: 2026-04-24

## Purpose

Pynapli needs a language system, not decorative slang.

The app should sound native to the lifestyle, clear to newcomers, restrained enough for mainstream app stores, and respectful enough to avoid feeling cheesy, explicit, spammy, or exploitative.

Core rule:

> Speak like a respectful insider, not a nightclub flyer.

## Product Language Goals

Pynapli language should be:

- Clear
- Mature
- Consent-forward
- Lifestyle-literate
- App-store-safe
- Inclusive of singles, linked partners, couples, and social users
- Useful for filtering and intent matching
- Easy for newcomers to learn without embarrassment

Pynapli language should not be:

- Porny
- Cheesy
- Wink-wink
- Overly explicit in public surfaces
- Pineapple-pun-heavy
- Escort-adjacent
- Judgmental
- Couple-centric by default

## Positioning Language

Use broad, safe language in public/app-store-facing surfaces.

Approved positioning phrases:

- Social lifestyle app
- Adult social lifestyle
- Open-minded adults
- Lifestyle community
- Location-based social discovery
- Partner linking
- Privacy-first discovery
- Social connections
- Events and meetups
- Singles and partnered people
- Connect solo, together, or socially

Avoid in public/app-store-facing surfaces:

- Hookup marketplace
- Sex app
- Find sex tonight
- Find swingers near you
- Swap partners tonight
- Naughty couples
- Hot singles and couples
- Adult fun near you
- Anything that implies escorting, compensation, or transactional arrangements

Recommended app-store-safe description style:

> Pynapli is a privacy-first social lifestyle app for open-minded adults. Discover nearby people, connect through the grid or swipe, link with a partner while keeping your own account, and choose whether you socialize solo, together, or at events.

## Glossary System

Pynapli should include an in-app glossary.

Users should be able to tap or long-press lifestyle terms in:

- Profile setup
- Filters
- Profile tags
- Discovery settings
- Event details
- Search results

The glossary should use short, neutral definitions.

Do not write definitions with jokes, innuendo, or moralizing.

## Glossary Definitions

Initial recommended definitions:

### Lifestyle

A broad term for consensual adult social and non-monogamous communities.

### ENM

Ethical non-monogamy; a relationship style where people have or are open to multiple romantic or intimate connections with everyone's knowledge and consent.

### Consensual Non-Monogamy

An umbrella term for relationship styles where partners agree that connections outside one relationship are allowed.

### Open Relationship

A relationship where partners agree that one or both people may connect with others outside the relationship.

### Swinging

A lifestyle where people, often couples, consensually socialize or connect with others outside their primary relationship.

### Soft Swap

Open to some shared intimacy, with limits agreed beforehand.

### Full Swap

Open to partner exchange when everyone involved consents.

### Same-Room

Prefer experiences where partners stay in the same room.

### Separate-Room

Open to partners being in separate spaces.

### New To Lifestyle

Still learning or early in the lifestyle community.

### Experienced

Comfortable with lifestyle spaces, norms, and communication.

### Unicorn

A single person, often a woman, who is open to meeting a couple.

### Stag

A partnered man who enjoys or supports his partner receiving outside attention.

### Vixen

A partnered woman who enjoys outside attention with her partner's knowledge and support.

### Hotwife

A partnered woman whose outside connections are known and supported by her partner.

### Bull

A man who connects with a partnered woman or couple in a mutually agreed dynamic.

### Bi

Attracted to more than one gender.

### Bicurious

Curious about attraction or connection with more than one gender.

### Kink-Friendly

Open to respectful conversations about kink, boundaries, and consent.

### Vanilla/Social

Interested in conventional social connection or lower-intensity lifestyle interaction.

### Meet & Greet

A social introduction, usually in a public or low-pressure setting.

### Host

Able or willing to host a meetup.

### Travel

Open to meeting outside one's immediate area or while visiting another location.

### Vetting

Taking time to confirm compatibility, safety, and mutual expectations before meeting.

### Boundaries

Personal limits or preferences that should be discussed and respected.

### Consent

Clear, voluntary agreement. Consent can be changed or withdrawn at any time.

### Hard Limits

Boundaries that are not open to negotiation.

### Soft Limits

Boundaries that may be discussed but still require clear agreement.

### Discretion

Respecting privacy and not sharing someone's identity, photos, or details without permission.

## Chat And Activity Language

Use neutral and low-drama copy.

Good:

- Alex viewed you
- Jamie sent a Wave
- Morgan sent a Spark
- Taylor requested album access

- Jamie left the chat
- Album access revoked

Avoid:

- Alex is watching you
- Jamie wants you badly
- Taylor wants your private pics
- Things are heating up
- Your partner joined the fun
- Someone is thirsty

## Empty States

Keep empty states useful and non-desperate.

Good:

- No one matches these filters right now.
- Try widening distance or turning off a dealbreaker.
- No new Activity yet.
- New views and flirts will appear here.
- No matches yet.
- Keep swiping or check the grid nearby.

Avoid:

- It's lonely here.
- No one wants to play yet.
- Your pineapple is waiting.
- Things are dry.

## Notification Language

Notifications must be subtle and privacy-conscious.

Good:

- You have new Activity.
- Someone viewed your profile.
- You have a new match.
- Alex sent a message.
- Jamie invited you to a chat.

Avoid:

- A hot couple wants you.
- Someone wants to swap.
- Your private album got attention.
- Lifestyle action nearby.
- Don't miss tonight's fun.

Because users may have privacy concerns, notification copy should avoid explicit lifestyle terms unless the user opts into detailed notifications.

## Safety And Consent Language

Safety copy should be direct and nonjudgmental.

Approved phrases:

- Consent can change at any time.
- Respect boundaries.
- Meet in public first if you prefer.
- Do not share photos or details without permission.
- Report behavior that violates community rules.
- Block removes mutual visibility where possible.
- Private album access can be revoked anytime.

Avoid:

- Play safe, naughty people.
- Be spicy but respectful.
- Don't be creepy.
- Drama-free only.

## App Store And Policy-Safe Language

Public metadata should emphasize:

- Adult social networking
- Privacy
- Partner linking
- Events
- Discovery
- Messaging
- Safety controls
- Verification

Avoid explicit sexual terminology in:

- App title
- Subtitle
- Screenshots
- App store description
- Push notification examples
- Public website hero copy

In-app settings can use more precise lifestyle terms after age-gated onboarding.

## Inclusive Language Rules

Pynapli is not only for heterosexual couples.

Use:

- Partner, not husband/wife by default
- Linked partner, not spouse
- People, members, users
- Singles and partnered people
- Linked people
- Couples only where the user explicitly selected that context

Avoid assuming:

- MF couples as default
- One man + one woman
- Couples always date together
- Linked means non-single in discovery mode
- Women are unicorns
- Men are bulls

## Taxonomy Governance

The taxonomy should be versioned and reviewed.

Each term should have:

- Internal key
- Display label
- Short definition
- Category
- App-store sensitivity level
- Whether it can be used in public copy
- Whether it can be used in push notifications
- Whether it is self-label only
- Whether it is filterable

Example:

```kotlin
data class LifestyleTerm(
    val key: String,
    val label: String,
    val definition: String,
    val category: TermCategory,
    val sensitivity: Sensitivity,
    val publicCopyAllowed: Boolean,
    val pushAllowed: Boolean,
    val selfLabelOnly: Boolean,
    val filterable: Boolean
)
```

## Do Not Forget

- Language is a UX feature.
- Keep public copy broad and safe.
- Use lifestyle terms only where they improve clarity.
- Define terms in-app.
- Avoid cheesy pineapple puns.
- Never assume linked means together.
- Never assume couples are heterosexual.
- Never make private album copy sound explicit or voyeuristic.
- Never make Activity or notification copy feel invasive.
- Let users self-label loaded terms.
