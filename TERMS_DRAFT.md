# Pynapli Terms Of Service Draft

Last updated: 2026-04-24

## Legal Review Required

This is a product/engineering draft, not final legal text. A lawyer must review before launch.

## Purpose

This draft defines the baseline product rules Pynapli must enforce.

## Eligibility

Pynapli is for adults 18+.

Users must:

- Be legally allowed to use the app.
- Provide accurate account information.
- Follow community guidelines.
- Respect consent and privacy.

## Account Ownership

Each account belongs to one person.

Rules:

- No shared couple accounts.
- No impersonation.
- No account selling/transferring.
- Users are responsible for activity on their account.

## Partner Linking

Partner linking is optional.

Rules:

- Both users must consent.
- Either user can unlink.
- Linking does not create shared account ownership.
- Linking does not grant automatic access to messages, albums, Activity, or settings.
- Linked partner subscription benefits end when link ends.

## User Content

Users are responsible for content they upload or send.

Pynapli may remove content that violates:

- Terms
- Community guidelines
- Law
- App-store requirements

Public content must remain app-store-safe.

## Prohibited Behavior

Not allowed:

- Underage use
- Harassment or threats
- Non-consensual content
- Doxxing
- Impersonation
- Spam/scams
- Paid sexual services
- Illegal activity
- Ban evasion
- Scraping or automation
- Interfering with app security

## Private Albums

Private albums are controlled by the owner.

Rules:

- Access can be revoked anytime.
- Access does not transfer to linked partners.
- Sharing or saving content without permission may violate guidelines and law.

## Subscriptions And Purchases

Pynapli offers:

- Free
- Social
- Select
- A la carte items such as Boosts, Pineapple Likes, Intro Notes, and Extra Picks

Rules:

- Entitlements are server-authoritative.
- Store billing rules apply.
- Linked partner benefits require active link eligibility.
- Abuse may result in loss of benefits.

## Safety Enforcement

Pynapli may:

- Remove content
- Limit features
- Require verification
- Suspend or ban accounts
- Preserve evidence
- Cooperate with legal requests where required

## Reports And Appeals

Users can report violations.

Some moderation decisions may be appealable. Severe legal/safety violations may not be appealable.

## Disclaimers

Pynapli does not guarantee:

- Matches
- Responses
- Compatibility
- Availability of specific users
- That users are truthful despite safety efforts

Users are responsible for their own offline decisions and safety.
