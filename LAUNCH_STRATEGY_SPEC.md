# Pynapli Launch Strategy Spec

Last updated: 2026-04-24

## Purpose

Pynapli is a network product. Launch strategy must create density, trust, and community legitimacy before broad scaling.

Core rule:

> Win one dense lifestyle market before expanding.

## Launch Thesis

Pynapli should launch city-by-city through trusted lifestyle communities and venues, not broad paid acquisition.

Recommended first market:

- Toronto / GTA / Ontario.

Reasons:

- Dense enough for grid.
- Manageable founder-led feedback.
- Existing lifestyle venues and communities.
- Good test market for singles, linked partners, and event use.

## Beta

Target:

- Invitation-only beta.
- Balanced singles and linked partners.
- 100+ early accounts.

Beta goals:

- Validate grid density.
- Validate linked partner flow.
- Validate Psync.
- Validate Playing Tonight.
- Validate private albums and safety controls.
- Validate moderation burden.

## Venue And Event Strategy

Use venue partnerships carefully:

- QR codes for signup.
- Event referral codes.
- Verified venue partner status.
- Pynapli Nights.
- Safety/moderation alignment.

Do not make the app dependent on one venue.

## Referral Strategy

Couple/linked referral:

- Linked users receive referral link/QR.
- Referred users get guided into onboarding and optional partner link flow.
- Rewards can be Social/Select trial days.

Solo referral:

- Individual referral link.
- Smaller reward.

Event referral:

- Venue/event-specific code.
- Tracks acquisition quality and conversion.

Use current tier names:

- Social.
- Select.

## Expansion Sequence

Possible sequence:

1. Toronto/GTA/Ontario.
2. Montreal, after French localization and Quebec privacy review.
3. New York City, after US legal/app-store review.
4. Los Angeles.
5. Other dense lifestyle cities.

Expansion trigger:

- Do not enter next market until current market has enough MAU, retention, safety operations, and grid density.

## Metrics

Track:

- MAU by market.
- Linked partner adoption.
- Grid open frequency.
- Grid message reply rate.
- Swipe match rate.
- Psync sessions completed.
- Playing Tonight activations.
- Day 7 retention.
- Day 30 retention.
- Referral share of signups.
- Social/Select conversion.
- Report rate.
- Moderation SLA.

## App Store Strategy

Prepare:

- Public community guidelines.
- Public privacy policy.
- Public safety/moderation explanation.
- App-store-safe screenshots.
- Age verification flow.
- Active moderation before submission.

Position:

- Social Networking or appropriate category after current review.
- Adult social lifestyle app.
- Privacy-first partner-linked discovery.

Avoid:

- Explicit sexual marketplace language.
- Screenshots showing explicit content.
- Escort/transactional framing.

## Do Not Implement

- Do not launch everywhere at once.
- Do not rely only on paid ads.
- Do not enter app stores without moderation and age-gate proof.
- Do not use outdated tier names in launch rewards.
- Do not make one venue the whole launch plan.
